<p class="lede">Research is how we make sure that the products, services, and policies we create truly work for the people of New Jersey. It grounds our decisions in the lived experiences of real users — not assumptions or internal perspectives.</p>

When we listen to people, we gain insight into what matters most to them, what challenges they face, and how government services can better meet their needs. This work helps us design better systems and builds trust by showing that residents' voices guide the process.

## Guiding principles

<p class="section-intro">All research must be rooted in trauma-informed, ethical, and inclusive practices.</p>

<div class="cols-3 compact-cards">
<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Safety" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Participants should feel emotionally, physically, and psychologically safe throughout the process. We ask what they need to feel comfortable and take steps to prevent harm or re-traumatization.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trust and transparency" icon="information">
    We explain our goals and methods in plain language, answer questions openly, and keep our commitments. Trust is built when expectations are clear and we follow through.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choice" icon="random">
    Participants have control over how they take part in research. We offer options, respect their decisions, and recognize that people who have experienced trauma may value control even more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collaboration" icon="puzzle">
    We aim to share power in the process, working alongside participants and partners rather than treating them as subjects.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Empowerment and voice" icon="rocket">
    We validate participants' experiences, invite them to shape outcomes, and support them in making decisions and taking action.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Equity and inclusion" icon="star">
    We recognize cultural, historical, and gender disparities, and actively work to reduce bias in our teams, methods, and recruitment. Diverse voices strengthen our work.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
</div>

## How we protect participants

<p class="section-intro">Whatever tools you use, these commitments hold for every study.</p>

<div class="cols-3 compact-cards">
<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Ask first" icon="approve-check">
    Get informed consent before every session, and offer a way to take part without being recorded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect less" icon="list-format">
    Only collect the personal information you need to answer your research question. Ask participants not to share or show personal information during sessions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Restrict access" icon="setting">
    Keep recordings and identifiable data in approved, access-restricted storage that only the research team can open.
  </Card>
  <Card title="De-identify before sharing" icon="document">
    Replace names with participant codes, and remove personal and health details before anything goes beyond the research team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete on schedule" icon="close">
    Tell participants how long you keep recordings, then delete them on time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extra care with health data" icon="warning">
    If a study could involve health information, follow HIPAA and your agency partner's data agreements, and design the study to avoid collecting it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
</div>
