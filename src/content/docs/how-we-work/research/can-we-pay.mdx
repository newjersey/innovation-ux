## How we decide

Whenever possible, we pay people who take time out of their lives to share their own experience in a personal capacity, such as a resident applying for unemployment benefits, an entrepreneur starting a business, or a disability insurance applicant testing a new application. We don't pay people who talk with us in a professional capacity or as part of their job, because they're usually already being paid for that time. Everyone who can be paid gets the same flat per-minute rate for their scheduled time.

Some people can still take part even though we can't pay them, and a few cases need a closer look first. When you're not sure, ask before the participant books, and write down what you decided and why.
